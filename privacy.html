Privacy policy

Your trip records stay with you.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

BagLedger is an offline packing and bag-weight planning app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains the app, the files you choose to share, support email, and this website separately.

1. Records in the app

Trips, bags, item names and quantities, weights, packing checks, kit items, and outbound history are stored in the app’s local storage on your device. The app does not require an account, operate its own cloud sync service, or automatically send these records to the developer. Device backups or storage services you choose through iOS are governed by your settings and those services.

2. No ads or remote analytics

The app contains no advertising or remote analytics. It does not request access to your location, contacts, or photo library for its packing workflow. It does not use tracking identifiers to build advertising profiles.

3. Import, export, and the system share sheet

You select a JSON file to import. A valid import is shown in a preview and, after confirmation, added as a new trip rather than replacing your existing records. Export produces a text checklist and a JSON trip backup. These files include trip and item data, weight records, and outbound history when present. They are created on your device. If you use the system share sheet, the destination you select receives the file; its own privacy policy and storage practices then apply.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete local trips and kit items in the app. Keep an exported backup outside the app before deleting data or uninstalling. Removing a local record does not remove copies you previously exported, shared, or included in a device backup; manage those copies at their destinations. The developer cannot retrieve a local-only record from a server.

5. Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to and investigate your request. Send only the information needed for the issue. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below; email service providers may retain routine delivery or backup records according to their practices.

6. This website and hosting

This website does not add analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising cookies, or a contact form. The hosting provider may process ordinary HTTP technical access records, such as IP address, requested page, browser information, and access time, to deliver and protect the site. Those website access records are separate from the trip data held locally by the app. Visiting this site does not upload your app’s trip records.

7. Questions and changes

Contact shuitian13@gmail.com for privacy questions. Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised date. Review the policies of any sharing, email, file-storage, or device-backup service you choose to use.
